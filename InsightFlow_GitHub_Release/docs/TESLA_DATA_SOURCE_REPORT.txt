TESLA, INC. — FIVE-YEAR BUSINESS & FINANCIAL DATA REPORT
Period: Fiscal years ended December 31, 2021 through December 31, 2025
Prepared: November 3, 2026
Currency: USD. Financial statement values are in USD millions unless the column name specifies otherwise.

IMPORTANT SCOPE AND DATA NOTES
This is a structured annual dataset for business-intelligence practice, not literally every disclosure in Tesla's 10-K filings. It includes selected consolidated financial statement lines, annual operating metrics, energy storage deployments, infrastructure counts, and calculated ratios. It is annual (five observations), not transaction-level or monthly data. Blank values mean not included/verified in this extract, not zero. The 2025 geographic revenue split is blank for that reason.
The data is intended for the InsightFlow prototype. With only five annual observations, forecasting is for demonstration only and is not statistically robust. Do not use it as investment advice.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
1. Revenue expanded from USD 53,823 million in 2021 to USD 94,827 million in 2025. The 2025 value was about 76.2% above 2021, but below 2024's USD 97,690 million.
2. Revenue peaked in this five-year window in 2024. It declined about 2.9% in 2025.
3. Vehicle deliveries rose from 936,222 in 2021 to 1,636,129 in 2025, but 2025 deliveries were below the 2023 and 2024 levels.
4. Energy storage deployments grew from 4.0 GWh in 2021 to 46.7 GWh in 2025, making energy generation and storage an increasingly important business line.
5. Energy generation and storage revenue increased from USD 2,789 million in 2021 to USD 12,771 million in 2025.
6. Gross profit declined from USD 20,853 million in 2022 to USD 17,094 million in 2025. Gross margin compression is visible in the calculated margin field.
7. Operating income decreased from USD 13,656 million in 2022 to USD 4,355 million in 2025. R&D spending increased to USD 6,411 million in 2025.
8. Net income attributable to common stockholders was unusually high in 2023 (USD 14,997 million); Tesla's 2024 annual report explains that 2023 net income included a substantial deferred-tax valuation allowance release. This makes the 2023 net margin less representative of recurring operating performance.
9. Operating cash flow remained strong at USD 14,747 million in 2025, while capital expenditure was USD 8,527 million. The calculated operating cash flow less capex was USD 6,220 million.

SUGGESTED INSIGHTFLOW QUESTIONS
- How did total revenue change from 2021 to 2025?
- Which revenue segment grew fastest?
- What happened to gross margin and operating margin?
- Compare vehicle production with deliveries each year.
- How quickly did energy storage deployments grow?
- How did R&D spending change relative to revenue?
- Compare operating cash flow with capital expenditures.
- Which year had the highest net margin, and why should it be interpreted carefully?

RECOMMENDED DASHBOARDS
A. Executive overview: revenue, gross profit, operating income, net income, cash flow and deliveries.
B. Segment mix: automotive, energy generation and storage, and services revenue.
C. Operations: production, deliveries, delivery/production ratio, storage deployed, charging network.
D. Profitability: gross margin, operating margin, net margin, R&D intensity.
E. Cash and investment: operating cash flow, capital expenditure, operating cash flow less capex, cash plus short-term investments.

METHODOLOGY
- Annual reported financial amounts are transcribed from Tesla's SEC Form 10-K annual reports.
- Annual vehicle production/delivery and energy storage deployment metrics are taken from Tesla Investor Relations annual operational summaries.
- Ratios and growth rates in the CSV are calculated from reported values.
- No fabricated monthly, customer-level, model-level revenue, share-price, or transaction data has been added.
